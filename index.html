HOW TO MEASURE

MEASURE YOUR BODY.

A / HEIGHT

Stand straight without shoes and measure from the floor to the top of your head.

B / WAIST

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist. Optional for KARAHEA's quick size recommendation.

C / HIP

Measure around the fullest part of your hips. Optional for a more precise recommendation.

D / WEIGHT

Use your current body weight. Height + weight are enough to get a starting size.
